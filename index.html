What does “previous generation” mean?

A prior production run of the same model. The differences from the current Mini are listed in “Compare with new”.

Can I see the unit in person?

Yes - book a time to inspect it at our warehouse before you reserve.

Can I add options?

Canopy, solar and freezer add-ons from the catalog can be installed before shipping; see “Complete your setup”.

What if it is damaged in transit?

Note damage on the delivery receipt and send photos within 48 hours; we handle the carrier claim.
